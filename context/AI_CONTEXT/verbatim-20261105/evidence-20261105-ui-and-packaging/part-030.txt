## Test Plan
- Adapter ingress：同一个请求从微信或语音进入，都能落到同一个 canonical session
- Domain SDK：`SignalsPack` 和 `DueDiligencePack` 通过同一套 contract test

- Failure drills：domain pack unavailable、partial evidence bundle、manual-review-required、remote worker unavailable、channel delivery denied
